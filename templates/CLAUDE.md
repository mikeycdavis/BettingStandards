# Working with the betting standards

generated by: standards init

This project is governed by a betting and gambling decision standards pack. Read this before
recommending, sizing, or recording any wager.

## The boundary

Whether a prediction deserves belief is not this pack's question. Whether that prediction, at the
offered price and under the applicable risk constraints, deserves money — that is.

A probability estimate is an INPUT here. Record where it came from; do not re-litigate it.

## The invariant

A good bet can lose. A bad bet can win. Bet quality is determined from information available at
decision time, never from the outcome. PASS is always a legitimate, successful decision.

## The workflow

1. `standards status .` — where things stand.
2. `standards plan .` — which rules apply, and what evidence each needs.
3. Build the decision record: price, full market, probability estimate with its source, uncertainty
   discount, bankroll, open positions, correlation group.
4. `standards check --record <draft.json> --dry-run` — evaluate BEFORE committing it to the ledger.
   The gates that fail are the reasons; if any fail, the decision is a PASS.
5. Write the record to the ledger.
6. `standards validate .` — the verdict.

## Rules you must not work around

- **Never edit a decision record after writing it.** Decision-time fields are covered by a digest.
  Settlement goes in the `outcome` block, which sits outside it. A correction is a NEW record
  referencing the original.
- **Never declare an exception against a prohibition.** It will be rejected and reported. If a
  prohibition genuinely has no subject here, use applicability with a reason.
- **On `BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT`, stop and report.** Do not reclassify the rule, lower a threshold,
  loosen a tolerance, or mark the rule not-applicable to clear the finding. Doing so is itself the
  behaviour `integrity.no-standard-weakening` prohibits.
- **Never report an unevaluated rule as passing.** Rules that prohibit motives cannot be checked
  mechanically; they report not-evaluated until a human attests. That is the correct answer.

## Conclusions available to you

`compliant`, `non-compliant`, `not applicable`, `insufficient evidence / not evaluated`, and
`blocked by invariant`.

You are never required to produce a positive recommendation. PASS is always available, there is no
bet quota, and a period with no wagers is a valid outcome. If the numbers do not support a wager,
say so — that is the system working.
